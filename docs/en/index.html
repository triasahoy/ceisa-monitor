CEISA Monitor
Version 2.0

All your customs documents,
in one dashboard.

A Chrome and Edge extension that reads your CEISA 4.0 document list with your own login session, then builds a summary, today's priorities, follow-ups, per-owner tasks, meeting decks, formal reports, Excel files and WhatsApp messages. Everything runs in the browser, with no server.

The 2.0 interface is in Indonesian; English returns in a later version.

What CEISA Monitor 2.0 does

All screens use fictitious data labelled DATA DEMO.

Summary with a conclusion

Open documents, SLA compliance, document age, and the status where documents are stuck, opened by a one-sentence conclusion.

Needs attention today

Red Lane documents still under inspection, documents older than 30 days, and the documents you watch.

Document panel

Stage timeline from the portal history with time in each status, document contents, duties, follow-up, and billing.

Reports that start from a purpose

Monthly meeting, daily update, archive and audit, or team tasks. The right files are selected and ready in seconds.

Per-owner report

A WhatsApp message and Excel file per person. The Result column your team fills in is imported back into follow-ups.

Meeting deck

About 15 slides with conclusion titles, a document-type comparison, findings, and decisions requested.

Install in two minutes

Chrome or Microsoft Edge 116 or later, only through the Chrome Web Store. Version 1 users are updated automatically and keep their data.

  1. Open CEISA Monitor on the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Click Add to Chrome, then pin the CEISA Monitor icon.
  3. Sign in to the CEISA 4.0 portal, open CEISA Monitor, and click Perbarui data (Refresh). Or choose Lihat mode demo (demo mode).

Secure by design

Details in SECURITY.md.

Read-only
No filling in or changing of documents.
No server
Data stays in your browser, separated per account.
No passwords
Tokens are not stored and sessions are not extended.
Minimal permissions
Host access only to portal.beacukai.go.id; notifications only if you allow them.
